About

Last updated: November 18, 2026

MediaFactory is a desktop application that runs on its owner's Windows PC. It turns gaming videos and stream highlights (made by the owner, or by creators who allow clips of their streams) into vertical clips with subtitles and on-screen text.

Clips are posted through the official TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API v3. For TikTok, every post is started by the account owner on the MediaFactory Publisher screen. On that screen the owner:

  • sees the nickname and avatar of the TikTok account that will post;
  • previews the video and edits the caption;
  • chooses who can view the video (no option is selected in advance);
  • turns comments, duets and stitches on or off;
  • discloses commercial content when applicable;
  • agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and clicks Post;
  • then sees the status of the post.

MediaFactory has no public sign-up and never posts to accounts its owner has not authorized.

Terms of Service

Effective: November 18, 2026

1. Who may use it

Only the owner of MediaFactory, with the TikTok and YouTube accounts the owner has authorized.

2. Content rules

  • Only content the owner created, or content the original creator allows to be clipped and edited, may be posted. Clips from other creators are modified (added text or music) and credit the creator.
  • Posts must follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Music Usage Confirmation and Branded Content Policy, and YouTube's Terms of Service.
  • The owner reviews each clip and decides privacy and interaction settings before it is posted to TikTok.

3. Authorization

Access to a TikTok account is granted through TikTok Login Kit. It can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

4. No warranty

MediaFactory is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.

5. Changes

These terms may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

What data is accessed

  • user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar of the authorizing account, shown on the publishing screen so the owner knows which account will post.
  • Creator settings from the Content Posting API (available privacy levels, whether comments, duets or stitches are turned off, maximum video length), used only to show the right options on the publishing screen.
  • video.publish: used only to post the clip the owner chose to that account.

How it is stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file excluded from version control. Post results (post ID, status, date) are kept in a local database on the same computer. The publishing screen runs only on that computer (localhost) and is not reachable from the internet.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs.

Retention and deletion

Revoking the app in TikTok stops all access immediately. The owner can delete the local token files and database at any time, which removes all stored data.
